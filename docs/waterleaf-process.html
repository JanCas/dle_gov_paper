Waterleaf / Lilac · Process

Intake screening → surge storage → raw-brine multimedia filtration. Native suspended material; raw-filter backwash routed through depleted-brine return. UF alternatives were tested. Selected commercial design supported by pilot/short cold-brine tests; no upstream dissolved-Mg/Ca removal claimed. O577, O578
