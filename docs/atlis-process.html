ATLiS · Process

HR1 post-secondary-clarifier brine → Fe/silica removal → Mn/Zn removal → ILiAD. Separated solids → filter-press dewatering. HCl pH control disclosed; limestone/CaO facilities listed. Detailed chemical sequence not disclosed. Commercial proposal; Fe/silica cake initially a waste, Mn/Zn intended products. No verified operating efficiency. O573, O574; O211.
