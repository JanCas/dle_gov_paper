Wellspring

Disclosure summary: government-hosted applicant proposal and high-level process drawing; no complete project-feed composition established. This profile covers a distributed field program, not a single fully identified commercial plant. Cutoff: September 16, 2026.

Triple 8 LLC, doing business as Wellspring Hydro, proposes produced-water DLE and carbonate validation with LibertyStream Infrastructure Partners. The August 31, 2025 application names more than ten North Dakota saltwater-disposal facilities but offers site details upon request. Earlier R-50-67 Prairie Lithium work is separate from the R-56-78 LibertyStream program. O528; D120.
