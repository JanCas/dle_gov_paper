South West Arkansas

The project is developed through SWA Lithium LLC, associated with Standard Lithium and Equinor under the Smackover Lithium name. The Reynolds Brine Unit and the central processing facility belong to the commercial project considered here. Standard Lithium’s demonstration plant at the LANXESS South site provides supporting test evidence; the LANXESS Phase 1A commercial project and East Texas projects are separate. D002, D035, D044.

The 2025 DFS describes selective sorption followed by concentration, impurity removal and conversion to lithium carbonate. The nominal design is 22,500 t/year, while the economic model’s lifetime average is 22,155.8 t (LCE)/year. The process-design table uses 439.9 mg/L lithium, while the economic summary uses 481 mg/L. Retaining both avoids accidentally treating a modelled production average as the process-design feed concentration. O001, O004, O005, O009, O014.
